## Why the route is evidence-relevant

The Bureau's official annual-reporting guidance says that electronic-cigarette
and refill-container reports include sales volumes and can be submitted through
EU-CEG. Its published reporting table specifies fields that can support a
same-country physical reconciliation:

- product ID, brand, brand subtype and product type;
- product units per pack;
- total e-liquid millilitres per product unit;
- devices and e-liquid containers inside a kit;
- sales volume as products or kits sold;
- sales year;
- retail or wholesale mode and market-research context.

The Bureau also publishes a market-monitoring route and annual survey reports.
Those studies are context evidence, not a substitute for the requested
administrative sales aggregate.

## Aggregate records requested

1. Annual 2018-2025 sales quantities by product type and presentation, including
   sold product or kit units, device count, refill-container count and total
   e-liquid millilitres where held.
2. National totals where product- or brand-level cells cannot be disclosed.
3. Reporting-entity, product and presentation coverage, missing or late
   reports, suppressed cells and revisions.
4. Definitions that distinguish retail and wholesale modes, reported sales,
   product notifications, market-monitoring studies and any modelled values.

## Evidence boundary

No response or data is publicly recorded from this supplementary request. The
official reporting duty establishes that a potentially useful administrative
source exists; it does not establish completeness, disclose the national
aggregate, or prove retail market value. Poland remains unaccepted at the donor
gate until the aggregate, coverage, measured supply-chain stage and a
same-boundary retail-value bridge are independently verified.

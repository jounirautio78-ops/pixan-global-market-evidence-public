# Poland 2020–2025 official-flow reconstruction

Reviewed 2026-07-31. This is a public-source reconstruction of physical and
fiscal evidence. It is not a consumer-retail market value, a donor-market
acceptance decision or a Pixan Oy statement.

## Direct official observations

The Polish Ministry of Finance response to parliamentary interpellation 7255
publishes national e-liquid volumes recorded from ZEFIR2 and the AIS import
system. The table describes domestic sales, intra-EU acquisitions and imports:

| Year | E-liquid volume |
| --- | ---: |
| 2020 | 1,451,529 litres |
| 2021 | 277,265 litres |
| 2022 | 416,088 litres |
| 2023 | 805,441 litres |

These are physical supply or release-flow quantities. They are not device
sales, consumer sell-through or observed retail value. The public response does
not provide the separate domestic-sale, intra-EU and import components,
coverage denominator, revision history or an independent reconciliation.

The Ministry of Health's earlier annual-report table adds producer/importer
reported quantities for six categories in 2019-2022:

| Category | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 preliminary |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Disposable e-cigarettes | 114,355 | 55,550 | 1,941,050 | 14,663,879 |
| Reusable e-cigarettes | 2,734,783 | 2,166,606 | 1,479,135 | 5,401,206 |
| Individual parts | 1,089,435 | 2,107,617 | 776,570 | 398,998 |
| Kits | 553,775 | 284,767 | 335,984 | 1,349,907 |
| Other | 470,642 | 336,532 | 243,782 | 227,957 |
| Refill containers/cartridges with e-liquid | 36,020,303 | 124,015,052 | 130,487,551 | 50,838,653 |

Not all required 2022 reports had been submitted. The preliminary disposable
count later increased to 19,525,600, a 4,861,721-unit or 33.15% revision. The
two values are revision controls and must not be summed. The producer/importer
transaction stage, consumer sell-through, channel coverage, prices, tax basis,
inventory and import de-duplication remain unestablished.

CMR separately estimates 2023 disposable-e-cigarette retail sales at about PLN
2 billion. The public article does not disclose the national sample frame,
weights, extrapolation, specialist-channel reconciliation or VAT/excise basis,
and the estimate excludes reusable devices, refill liquids and pods. It is a
commercial segment check, not a whole-market value or donor.

The Ministry response to interpellation 17526 publishes realised 2025 excise:

| Product | Realised 2025 excise |
| --- | ---: |
| E-liquid | PLN 993.1 million |
| Broad vaporisation-device group | PLN 175.3 million |
| Component sets for the broad group | PLN 2.5 million |

The Polish Ministry of Finance rate page records a PLN 40 per-unit excise for
the broad vaporisation-device group and PLN 40 per set from 1 July 2025.
Deterministic division gives:

- broad-group devices: PLN 175,300,000 / PLN 40 =
  **4,382,500 implied taxed units**;
- broad-group component sets: PLN 2,500,000 / PLN 40 =
  **62,500 implied taxed sets**.

These are tax-base bridges for the period in which the new taxes applied. They
are not full-year sell-through. Cash timing, refunds, exports, destruction,
stock movements and the relationship between taxed units and retail
transactions remain unreconciled.

The official `urządzenia do waporyzacji` category is not e-cigarette-only. It
includes refillable electronic cigarettes, heated-tobacco heaters and
multifunction devices. The AKC-4/R declaration requires those types to be
identified separately, but the current aggregate does not publish that split.
The 4,382,500 devices and 62,500 component sets therefore cannot be described
as e-cigarette-only counts.

No single e-liquid quantity is back-solved from the PLN 993.1 million receipt:
the 2025 rate changed during the year and disposable products acquired an
additional per-device charge. A single-rate division would therefore create a
false precision.

## Donor-readiness boundary

Poland now has a reproducible multi-year e-liquid flow series and a 2025
device/component tax-base bridge, but it is not yet a donor candidate for a
full-year national consumer-retail market value.

The direct official evidence supports:

- a complete identified year for each published volume;
- separate e-liquid and broad device/component tax categories, with the
  remaining e-cigarette/heater/multifunction split explicitly identified;
- public reproducibility from official sources.

The following remain blocking:

- consumer-retail transaction value rather than supply or tax stage;
- devices and consumables within the same full-year boundary;
- the AKC-4/R split between refillable e-cigarettes, heaters and
  multifunction devices;
- nationwide retail-channel coverage and missingness;
- VAT/excise-inclusive or exclusive retail price basis;
- a non-duplicative bridge across domestic sales, intra-EU acquisitions,
  imports, refunds, exports and inventories;
- independent same-year reconciliation.

Until those items are closed, Poland remains a high-priority evidence programme
and not an accepted donor. It does not change the public 0/3 donor gate or
release a global estimate.

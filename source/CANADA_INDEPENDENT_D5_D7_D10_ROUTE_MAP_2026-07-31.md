# Canada independent D5/D7/D10 route map

Reviewed: 2026-07-31
Current candidate: Canada 2024, **7/10, not accepted**

## Decision

The new routes below strengthen triangulation but do not close the current
national donor gaps. D5 and D7 remain `failed`; D10 remains `open`. Canada's
official retail point estimate remains CAD 1,219,160,000
(EUR 822,583,715.21), but it is not eligible for the global roll-up.

No request, purchase, licence, fee acceptance or external message was made in
preparing this route map. Statistics Canada has already confirmed that the
missing product-class quality fields are unavailable, so no further
Statistics Canada follow-up is scheduled.

## Ranked independent routes

### 1. Obtain the already processed Health Canada ATI package

Completed request `A-2024-000236` covers the final report and related results
from Health Canada's commissioned “Study of the Market Size, Characteristics
and Growth Trends of the Vaping Products Market in Canada and the Provinces”.
The official ATI record identifies 106 pages and a `disclosed in part`
disposition.

This is the fastest potentially information-rich route because the access
review has already been completed. A no-charge informal copy request should be
tried before a new ATIP request or paid market-data purchase. The underlying
study was compiled by Euromonitor International, so it is not fully independent
of that vendor and any reuse or publication rights must be checked before a
claim enters a lender package.

Official record:
https://search.open.canada.ca/ati/record/hc-sc,2024,11,A-2024-000236

Health Canada's public discussion paper cites the same 2024 commissioned study
and states that online sales were estimated at about 23% of the Canadian vaping
market, with 33% projected for 2028. These figures are contextual until the
report's definitions, year, product scope and rights are inspected:
https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/programs/consultation-third-legislative-review-tobacco-vaping-products-act/document.html

### 2. Reconstruct provincial taxable retail bases

Newfoundland and Labrador applies a 20% vapour-products tax to the purchase
price at retail. Its cash statement reports CAD 7.582 million for fiscal
2024–25. The mechanical taxable-purchase-price base is therefore:

`CAD 7,582,000 / 20% = CAD 37,910,000`

The official Health Canada calendar-2024 net-shipment value for Newfoundland
and Labrador is CAD 19,497,599.98. The unmatched ratio is 1.9443x. This is a
useful provincial reconciliation lead, not a national retail estimate.

Sources:

- https://www.gov.nl.ca/fin/tax-programs-incentives/business/vapour-tax/
- https://www.gov.nl.ca/exec/tbs/files/Cash-Statements-2025-with-signed-letter-Final.pdf
- https://health-infobase.canada.ca/substance-use/vaping/sales/

Saskatchewan applies a 20% vapour-products tax. The province reports
CAD 11,313,550 of vapour-products tax for fiscal 2024–25. The mechanical
taxable-selling-price base is:

`CAD 11,313,550 / 20% = CAD 56,567,750`

The official Health Canada calendar-2024 net-shipment value for Saskatchewan is
CAD 30,454,189.17. The unmatched ratio is 1.8575x.

Sources:

- https://www.saskatchewan.ca/business/taxes-licensing-and-reporting/provincial-taxes-policies-and-bulletins/vapour-products-tax
- https://www.saskatchewan.ca/business/taxes-licensing-and-reporting/provincial-taxes-policies-and-bulletins/tax-revenue-figures
- https://health-infobase.canada.ca/substance-use/vaping/sales/

Both calculations mix a fiscal-year tax receipt with a calendar-year shipment
control. Product scope, exemptions, refunds, cash timing, inventory, margins
and non-filing must be reconciled. They do not pass D10 by themselves.

### 3. Obtain the aggregate statutory duty field

Section 4 of the federal Vaping Products Reporting Regulations requires
reporting of both:

- net sales excluding taxes and duties by province and Canada; and
- the Canadian-dollar value of federal vaping duty and additional vaping duty,
  net of returns, by province and Canada.

The public Health Canada sales download exposes net sales but not the aggregate
duty field. An existing national and provincial 2024 aggregate of that field
would provide a direct tax bridge without asking reporters for new data.

Official regulation:
https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-123/FullText.html

### 4. Retain the customs triangle as a border-stage control

The Canadian Importers Database reports calendar-2024 imports of:

| HS6 | 2024 import value |
|:---|---:|
| 240412 | CAD 576,672,945 |
| 240419 | CAD 3,130,942 |
| 854340 | CAD 95,052,463 |
| **Total** | **CAD 674,856,350** |

The combined import value is 58.14% of Health Canada's 2024 manufacturer /
importer net shipments and 55.35% of Statistics Canada's 2024 retail point
estimate. This is a customs-value control, not consumer retail sales. It is
not adjusted for domestic production, low-value shipments, re-exports,
inventory, duty, tax, wholesale or retail margins.

Official reports:

### 5. Use channel and enforcement sources only as structure evidence

Health Canada's public discussion paper identifies specialty stores,
convenience stores and online sales, and cites the commissioned study's
online-channel share. Health Canada's April 2024–March 2025 enforcement report
records inspections at 546 specialty vaping establishments, 2,136 gas and
convenience stores where vaping products were inspected, and 124 online
inspections in the wider review record. The programme is risk- and
priority-based; the inspection counts are not an outlet census or sales
weights.

Sources:

- https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/smoking-tobacco/vaping/compliance-enforcement/report-april-2024-march-2025.html
- https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/programs/consultation-third-legislative-review-tobacco-vaping-products-act/final-report.html

The 2022 Canadian Tobacco and Nicotine Survey public-use file can support
consumer purchase-channel structure. Multi-select purchase-place responses
must not be treated as mutually exclusive market shares or spend weights.

## Recommended order

1. Ask for a copy of the already processed ATI package `A-2024-000236` and
   inspect its definitions and rights before considering paid data.
2. Seek existing calendar-2024 monthly taxable-sales or tax aggregates from
   Newfoundland and Labrador and Saskatchewan, plus the exact adjustment
   fields needed for reconciliation.
3. Seek the existing aggregate VPRR duty field by province and Canada.
4. Keep customs, survey-channel and inspection evidence as labelled
   triangulation only.
5. Re-score Canada only after a rights-cleared route quantitatively bounds the
   missing specialist / online channel and independently reconciles the
   retail-to-shipment residual.

# Third-donor acquisition screen — 2026-07-27

This is a source-acquisition ranking, not a D1–D10 assessment. None of the 15 countries is accepted as a donor market, and the public donor gate remains 0/3.

## Decision

1. **Primary programme — Poland.** Official sources publish national e-liquid
   flows for 2020–2023 and realised 2025 e-liquid and broad
   vaporisation-device-group excise. The PLN 40 rates reproduce a second-half
   tax-base bridge of 4,382,500 devices and 62,500 component sets, but the
   official group mixes refillable e-cigarettes, heated-tobacco heaters and
   multifunction devices. These are not e-cigarette-only units or full-year
   consumer-retail sales. A formal D1–D10 preassessment now records D2 and D4
   as failed, D9 as passed for the current anchors and the other seven
   criteria as open; it assigns no donor score because no country-year retail
   value exists.
2. **Source-only lead — Russia.** A new official report exposes marked retail value and liquid litres, but devices, the illegal-market boundary, reproducibility, sanctions and operational constraints prevent it from becoming the primary programme.
3. **Secondary official-request wave — Finland, Denmark and France.** Each has a credible statutory or regulator-held annual reporting route, but a public national retail aggregate and complete value bridge remain missing.

The remaining monitored routes are the United Arab Emirates, China, United Kingdom, United States, Netherlands, Italy, Spain, Sweden, Philippines and Saudi Arabia. Their precise official sources and blocking gaps are stored in `third-donor-screen.json`.

## Fail-closed boundary

- The screen does not itself assign a retail value. The separate Poland reconstruction publishes only direct official volume, realised excise and an explicitly derived tax-base bridge.
- It does not assign a donor score. The separate Poland preassessment records
  evidence states and blockers without adding a sixth donor candidate.
- It does not change New Zealand or Canada.
- It does not authorise a data purchase or external message.
- Russia is not treated as a transaction-ready or operationally preferred route.

The public machine-readable record and its schema are the controlling source for the dashboard.

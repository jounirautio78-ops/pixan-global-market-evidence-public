## What the official response establishes

The official response establishes a narrow records boundary:

1. Obliged inhalation-liquid taxpayers submit periodic summaries of release for
   consumption.
2. Since 2022 the declarations have been acquired through ADM's technology
   partner and are available to the tax administration for taxpayer-control
   purposes.
3. The responding financial-administration function stated that it does not
   hold those declarations and does not produce the requested aggregate
   processing.
4. ADM identified public fiscal, statistical and registry routes instead.

The response did **not** supply the requested annual or monthly millilitres,
retail value, sold device units, sold liquid volume, domestic-release bridge,
imports, intra-EU acquisitions, refunds, exports or an annual market total.

## Public fiscal anchor - not a market-size observation

ADM's *Libro Blu 2024*, table III.9, reports consumption-tax revenue for
`prodotti liquidi da inalazione`:

| Measure | 2023 | 2024 | Published change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Consumption-tax revenue | EUR 55,910,871.89 | EUR 84,309,841.41 | +50.79% |

The table is an official tax-revenue anchor only.

- It is not consumer retail value.
- It is not physical volume in millilitres.
- It is not a count of devices, pods, cartridges or refill containers.
- The published percentage is growth in tax revenue, not market growth.
- The 2024 fiscal scope and rates changed. The revenue movement therefore
  cannot be converted into volume or like-for-like market growth without a
  dated rate-and-scope bridge, refunds, credits, destruction, exports and
  inventory timing.
- It is not donor evidence and is excluded from the global retail-value rollup.
